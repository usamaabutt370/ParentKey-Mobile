Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 27, 2026

ParentKey ParentKey Child

This Privacy Policy describes how ParentKey (“we,” “us,” or “ParentKey”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use:

  • ParentKey (Android package: com.parentkey.parent) — the parent app
  • ParentKey Child (Android package: com.parentkey.child) — the child/device app

Together, these are the “Apps.” This single policy covers both Apps and related services (the “Service”).

By creating an account, pairing a device, or using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Apps.

1. Who this Service is for

ParentKey is a parental-control Service. The parent/guardian creates an account and manages linked children’s devices. The child uses ParentKey Child on a device that the parent has linked.

We design the Service so that parents understand and control monitoring features. Children (or the person setting up the child device) are shown an in-app explanation of what parents can see before continuing setup.

If you are a parent or guardian in the United States and your child is under 13, you are responsible for providing appropriate parental consent before collecting or sharing your child’s information through the Service, consistent with applicable law (including COPPA).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information provided by parents

  • Account credentials (email address and password)
  • Name (first, last, and/or full name)
  • Child account details you create or manage (child name, optional age, avatar choice, and credentials if you add a child manually)
  • App block / limit rules you set for a child’s device
  • Settings such as whether uninstalling the child app is allowed

2.2 Information provided on the child device

  • Child profile information entered during setup (name, age, optional profile photo)
  • Consent acknowledgment shown during child setup
  • Pairing information when linking to a parent (for example via QR code)

2.3 Device, usage, and monitoring data (primarily via ParentKey Child)

To provide parental controls, the child app may collect and sync:

  • Device identifiers used for pairing and device records (for example Android device ID, or a generated device key)
  • Device platform and a device label
  • Installed application inventory (app names, package names, categories, system-app flags, and app icons)
  • Daily foreground app usage (time spent in apps)
  • Last-seen / sync timestamps and when usage tracking started
  • Push notification tokens (for example FCM tokens) used to trigger rule/sync updates

On Android, ParentKey Child may request sensitive system permissions (such as Usage Access, Accessibility, Device Admin, overlay/display-over-other-apps, notifications, and battery optimization exemptions) so it can measure usage, enforce blocks, and sync in the background. On iOS, Screen Time / Family Controls permissions may be used for similar enforcement where available.

2.4 Camera and photos

  • Camera: used to scan a parent pairing QR code and, optionally, to take a profile photo
  • Photo library / media: used if the child chooses an existing profile photo

We do not use the camera for continuous recording or background surveillance.

2.5 Automatically collected technical data

  • Authentication session data stored locally on the device
  • Basic operational data needed to run sync, pairing, and account features

We do not currently use separate advertising SDKs or third-party marketing analytics in the Apps.

2.6 Information we do not intentionally collect

As of the effective date above, the Apps are not designed to collect:

  • Precise GPS location / continuous location tracking
  • Contacts, SMS content, call logs, or microphone audio for monitoring
  • Payment card details (no in-app payment processing is implemented at this time)

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate parent and child accounts
  • Pair child devices with a parent account
  • Show parents installed apps, usage summaries, and linked devices
  • Apply and sync app blocking / limiting rules to child devices
  • Store and display child profile information and avatars
  • Send operational push messages that help the child app sync rules and status
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How parent and child data is shared within the Service

Linked parents can view and manage information associated with their children’s accounts and devices, including profile details, installed apps, usage summaries, devices, and block rules. Child devices receive rules and settings configured by the linked parent.

Pairing uses short-lived pairing sessions (for example QR tokens) so a child device can be securely linked to a parent account.

5. Third-party service providers

We use trusted processors to operate the Service. These providers process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, realtime updates, and file storage (including child avatar images)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — push/sync messaging for the child app
  • Expo / EAS — app build and distribution tooling

These providers may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies and our configuration. We do not sell personal information.

6. Children’s privacy

ParentKey Child is intended to be used under parental/guardian supervision as part of a family parental-control setup. Parents should only enroll a child’s device if they have authority to do so and have explained monitoring to the child as appropriate.

We collect child-related information only to provide parental-control features requested by the parent (account/profile data, device inventory, usage, and enforcement settings).

Parents can delete a linked child account from the parent app. A child account can also be deleted from the child app. If you believe we have collected a child’s information inappropriately, contact us at info@devorium.com and we will take reasonable steps to investigate and delete it.

7. Data retention

We retain account, device, usage, and rule data for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes (for example security, dispute resolution, and compliance).

  • Pairing sessions expire automatically after a short period (about 15 minutes).
  • When a parent or child deletes a child account, associated child account data is removed from our systems subject to normal deletion/cascade processes and any legally required retention.
  • When a parent deletes their own account in-app, linked child accounts and related data are also deleted.
  • Signing out of an app removes the local session but does not by itself delete server-side account data.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal information, or to object to certain processing.

  • Parents: you can update profile information in-app, manage child accounts, change block rules, delete linked child accounts, and delete your parent account from Settings.
  • Account deletion: parents can delete their account (and all linked child accounts) from ParentKey Settings. Children can delete their child account from ParentKey Child. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Permissions: you can revoke device permissions in system settings; some features will stop working if required permissions are withdrawn.

To exercise privacy rights, contact info@devorium.com. We may need to verify your identity and authority (especially for child-related requests).

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), authenticated API access, and access controls in our backend. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

10. International transfers

We may process and store information on servers operated by our providers (such as Supabase and Google) that may be located outside your country of residence. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

11. Data sharing disclosures

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers who help us operate the Service (see Section 5)
  • To the linked parent/guardian as part of the product’s intended function
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality
  • With your direction or consent

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

12. Third-party links and platforms

The Apps may interact with platform services (Google Play, Apple, device OS permission screens). Those services are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for third-party practices outside our Service.

13. Changes to this policy
