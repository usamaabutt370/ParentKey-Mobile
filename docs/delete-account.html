Delete your ParentKey account

Last updated: July 27, 2026

ParentKey ParentKey Child

Use this page to delete your ParentKey or ParentKey Child account and associated data. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

ParentKey (parent app)

  1. Open ParentKey and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account in the Danger zone.
  4. Confirm deletion.

Deleting a parent account also deletes all linked child accounts and related device, usage, and blocking data.

ParentKey Child (child app)

  1. Open ParentKey Child and sign in.
  2. Scroll to the Danger zone.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm deletion.

A parent can also delete a linked child account from the parent app under the child’s details.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app option (for example, you uninstalled the app), email us to request deletion:

Email: info@devorium.com

Include:

  • The email address used for the account
  • Whether the account is for ParentKey or ParentKey Child
  • A clear request to delete the account and associated data

We will verify ownership and process deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible, typically within 30 days.

3. What data is deleted

When an account is deleted, we remove associated data such as:

  • Account login and profile information
  • Linked child accounts (when a parent account is deleted)
  • Device pairing records, app block rules, and synced usage data
  • Child profile photos stored for the account

4. What may be retained

We may retain limited information only when required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting reasons. Signing out of an app removes the local session but does not delete your account.
